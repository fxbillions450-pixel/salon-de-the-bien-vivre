# Content Checklist — Salon de Thé Bien Vivre

This checklist covers all photos, copy, and content needed before launch.

---

## Photos Required

### Hero & General
- [ ] **Hero image** — wide-format interior shot of the tea salon (1920×1080px min)
- [ ] **About hero** — team photo or atmospheric interior shot
- [ ] **Gallery** — minimum 12 high-quality photos of the space, food, drinks, events

### Menu & Food
- [ ] Tea selection flat lay or styled photo
- [ ] Matcha preparation / matcha latte
- [ ] Fresh juice selection
- [ ] Vegan meals (at least 3–4 dishes)
- [ ] Pastries / desserts (at least 3–4 items)
- [ ] Menu category cover images (8 categories)

### Experiences & Workshops
- [ ] Yoga session in the space
- [ ] Pilates / meditation session
- [ ] Painting workshop
- [ ] Plant / bracelet workshop
- [ ] Pop-up market atmosphere
- [ ] Individual instructor headshots

### Private Events
- [ ] Private event setup photo (decorated space)
- [ ] Corporate event or group gathering

### Exterior & Branding
- [ ] Exterior / storefront photo
- [ ] Logo (SVG + PNG, light and dark versions)
- [ ] Favicon (32×32, 64×64)
- [ ] OG image for social sharing (1200×630px)

## Copy Required (French + English)

### Homepage
- [ ] Hero tagline and subtitle
- [ ] About section short paragraph (2–3 sentences)
- [ ] Experience category descriptions (yoga, meditation, painting, etc.)
- [ ] Alert bar message (if used)

### About Page
- [ ] Full salon story / mission (3–5 paragraphs)
- [ ] Team member bios (name, role, short bio, photo)
- [ ] Values section (3–4 values with descriptions)

### Menu Page
- [ ] All menu items: name, description, price, allergens, dietary tags
- [ ] Seasonal specials (if any)

### Experiences Page
- [ ] Cancellation policy text
- [ ] What to bring / prepare text (can be per-experience)
- [ ] Each experience: title, description, duration, price, capacity, instructor

### Private Events Page
- [ ] Event type options list (corporate, birthday, bridal, etc.)
- [ ] Budget range options
- [ ] Venue capacity information
- [ ] Rental terms / general conditions

### Vendor / Pop-up Page
- [ ] Market concept description
- [ ] Vendor criteria / what you look for
- [ ] Space/table details

### FAQ Page
- [ ] Minimum 10 frequently asked questions with answers

### Legal Pages
- [ ] Privacy Policy (politique de confidentialité) — complete French version
- [ ] Terms of Use (conditions d'utilisation) — complete French version
- [ ] Cancellation Policy (politique d'annulation) — complete text

### Contact Page
- [ ] Physical address confirmation
- [ ] Phone number
- [ ] Email address
- [ ] Map embed or directions
